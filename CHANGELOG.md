## 1.0.0 - 2026-09-25

### Added

- Frozen three-case research pipeline evaluating model self-explanation, controlled input intervention, Integrated Gradients, and activation patching against a shared logit-difference target score.
- Reproduction entrypoint (`python -m mth110.experiment`) persisting case evidence and environment metadata to `data/frozen.json`.
- Research protocol specification and coursework documentation.
